Narrate a document

Paste text or load a .txt or .md file. Start each chapter with a line such as # Chapter title (one or two # signs). Each chapter becomes a separate audio file in the Library, so a long job can run overnight and be played chapter by chapter. A job saves every part as it is made and can be stopped and resumed. While a job runs, Breeze occupies the GPU: Open WebUI and agent requests wait 2 minutes and then fail, so avoid them until the job is done or stopped.
